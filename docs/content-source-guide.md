# MONAURO Content Source Guide

## Rule

If a claim needs proof, it must come from a source file before it appears as final website copy.

## Suggested Source Folders

```text
product-data/
product-images/
brand-assets/
reviews-and-proof/
policies/
seo-content/
shopify-data/
```

## Product Data

Required fields:

- Product name
- SKU
- Shopify handle
- Price
- Dimensions
- Weight
- Materials
- Color options
- Battery and charging details
- Modes and heat levels
- Package contents
- Usage guidance
- Safety warnings

## Images

Use real or approved product assets for:

- White background product images
- Detail images
- Packaging images
- In-use images
- Scenario images

Do not use AI-generated images for exact product structure, dimensions, materials, certification marks, or packaging claims.

## Reviews And Proof

Only publish reviews, KOL quotes, expert notes, media mentions, or customer stories when MONAURO has permission and source records.

Keep:

- Reviewer name or approved display name
- Region if allowed
- Product used
- Original review text
- Permission status
- Screenshot or source URL

## Policies

Confirm before publishing:

- Shipping regions and timeframes
- Return window
- Warranty period
- Privacy policy
- Terms of service
- Disclaimer
- Support email

## SEO Content

Recovery Library articles should include sources or a review boundary, especially for health-related topics.

Good article directions:

- Heavy legs after sitting
- Calf tightness after running
- Compression vs massage
- Heat and everyday muscle relaxation
- Digital eye strain routines

Avoid medical diagnosis or treatment instructions.
