FAQ

Safety, usage, policy, and checkout questions.

Does this replace medical treatment?+

No. RecoveryAir Calf Pro is described as a home electronic wellness device, not a medical device. It is not intended for diagnosis or treatment and should not replace professional medical advice.

How do I use RecoveryAir Calf Pro?+

Place the massager on your calves, secure it with the Velcro straps, adjust to a comfortable tightness, then select the massage intensity and heat setting. Remove jewelry and accessories before use, and do not operate the product with wet hands.

Can I use it while charging?+

No. The manual states that the device cannot be powered on while charging.

Is it waterproof?+

No. The manual states that the product is not waterproof and should not be splashed with liquid or used on wet skin.

What is included in the box?+

RecoveryAir Calf Pro includes the main device, charging cable, user manual, warranty card, and Certificate of Conformity.

What is the warranty policy?+
